## Ideation

Late october of 2025, I realized a couple of things:

- I've got exactly 19 days of paid leaves left for the year, enough exactly for 3 weeks, plus weekends either side
- My next birthday (in Jan, 2026) would be by 23<sup>rd</sup>
- Typhoid made me realize you indeed only live once
- There wasn't anything particularly stupid I had done in 2025, meaning I was overdue
- Murphy was with me, had gone on quite long trips with her
- The helmet had a bluetooth music player in it, the GoPro was attached
- Had already ordered Toothless (my DJI Mini 4 Pro) to take to Travis Scott's Circus Maximus Mumbai Tour

I don't know about you, but this sounded like a recipe for disaster and an adrenaline fueled adventure. And that's when it popped into my mind. Time for a drive.

## Route Planning

The first thing to be done, is obviously look at my financials and if the trip I had been subconciously brewing in my mind was even close to practical feasability or not. (Spoiler alert: It was absolutely fucking not.)

So, as any other sane person, I ordered a political map of India (100 x 140 cm) from Amazon and sticky notes from Blinkit. You already know where this is going, don't you? Yeah, I hung the map on my bedroom wall and plastered the places I wanted to visit on it. The next step was something I had worked on since I was 5: *Join the dots*

Did some research on google maps estimating the time it'd take for me to go from one place to another, made a [rudimentary doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1D-oKp7zgjuFRTzjqV4DtnqHI3PCWBUccHCRNsy4lC1Y/edit?usp=sharing), fed it into ChatGPT, asked it about my plan, got excited that it told me I was crazy, and discussed it with my friend, Nabeel, at work.

I settled finally on leaving home around 6 PM, Friday, Nov 28<sup>th</sup>, 2025 and targetting reaching home by 6 PM, Sunday, Dec 21<sup>st</sup>, 2025. Exactly 23 days. Giving the project it's dumb yet iconic name: `23 Before 23`

## Preparing Murphy

My roadster was not equipped in it's current state for a trip of that scale. I went to the dealership to get some things installed on her already perfect body. There were 4 things I had in mind that I knew were going to be bare minimum required:

1. Handle guards (thank god)
2. Rear wheel mud flap
3. Fog lights
4. Rear top box stand

At the dealership, they did have the handle guards and mud flap, the top box stand would come in 10-12 days (it didn't eventually), but no fog lights were available for Murphy. Me, being naive and new to the world of motorcycles, asked them to just put on the handle guards and the mud flap in the morning and took her back home in the evening. Fortunately, they had my permanent bike registration number too, so they attached it. I'm pretty confident that I'd have driven across India with my temporary number had it come to that! XD

Now comes the stupidity part, because no *good* story is complete without one. It was the afternoon of Dec 27 and I still didn't have confirmation on the arrival of the the rear top-box stand. Plus, I still had to figure out the fog light situation, as my plan had some night rides too.

Fortunately, I met a guy at the dealership, who also owned a Guerilla 450 (had come to get his serviced there), that Karol Bagh had terrific moto shops (both bike and cars). Since I had work from home, I rode till there, got my top-box stand and fog lights attached within an hour and drove back home to clock-out for the day :P. On my way back, I stopped at the last fuel station to make sure that Murphy is well fed for tomorrow.

Oh, by the way, the Mumbai Trip (Travis Scott concert) was from Nov 19-23 and on top of that, my work friends planned an impromptu 2 day Rishikesh trip for Nov 25-26. So, yeah, these were the conditions I had to plan my almost-month-long-solo-across-india bike trip, for which I had to leave on the evening of Nov 28. Yeah, good luck me.

## Finishing touches

Now, I knew that I'd be taking my bagpack with me since the bike's top-box would be too heavy to handle my reckless driving style (turned out true), and I'd atleast be able to wear it in case something happens to my top-box stand (again, true). But, I was quite certain that I would not be able to fit all my stuff in a single bagpack. Plus, I'd need to access stuff like my phone, GoPro, Toothless, wallet, papers, etc quite frequently. So I decided to order a couple of items:

- 18L Tank bag (non-magnetic)
- ND filters for Toothless
- Protective casing for the GoPro
- Motorbike Luggage straps (2 sets of 2)
- GoPro helmet mount
- Print-outs of my documents:
    - Aadhar Card
    - Driving License
    - Registration Certificate
    - Bike's Insurance
- Electronics
    - Wireless charger cum power bank (10k mAh)
    - 3-in-1 fast (67W) charger
    - 100W C-to-C data cables for charging stuff
    - 1TB external SSD
    - 10Gbps C-to-C cable for fast media copy
    - 256GB microSD card

Like a sane person, I didn't order all of them at once. Some were done from Mumbai, some from Rishikesh, and some a day before I was gonna leave. Lo and behond, it was already Nov 28, the big day!
